Privacy by Design Guarantee: This application never asks for or stores actual phone numbers, passwords, exact addresses, or private messages. We never scrape real profiles. Only self-reported configurations and derived scores are processed.
Privacy
Data Control

Controls how personal information is collected, exposed, shared, and used. Focuses on data minimization, audience boundaries, and restricting public accessibility of biographical details.

Example: Hiding phone number, birth year, and residential neighborhood from public view.
Security
Access Protection

Protects systems, accounts, and information from unauthorized access or compromise. Focuses on multi-factor authentication, strong unique passwords, and active session management.

Core Principle: Strong Account Security ≠ Strong Privacy. An account can have a 25-character password and MFA, yet still publicly leak home location and phone numbers!

10 Comprehensive Risk Categories Evaluated

Our 45-question diagnostic evaluates exposure across the entire social footprint:

Profile Visibility

Search engine indexation, public discoverability via phone/email, and connection list visibility.

Personal Information

Public exposure of phone numbers, emails, full birth dates, and family relationships.

Location Privacy

Real-time check-ins, travel itinerary broadcasting, routine route disclosures, and EXIF leaks.

Posts & Content

Workplace ID badges, corporate desk screens, minor identification, and boarding pass barcodes.

Friends & Followers

Acceptance of unknown connection requests, lack of connection pruning, and follower engagement pods.

Tagging & Mentions

Timeline review queues, third-party photo tagging permissions, and unsolicited public handle mentions.

Authentication & Security

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), password reuse across services, and unrecognized login alerts.

Third-Party Apps

Stale OAuth authorizations, viral quiz scopes, direct message access, and least-privilege review.

Social Engineering

OTP sharing awareness, DM phishing links, viral security-question quizzes, and urgent wire requests.
