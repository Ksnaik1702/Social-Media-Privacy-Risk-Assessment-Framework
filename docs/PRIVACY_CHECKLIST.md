# Comprehensive Social Media Privacy & Hardening Checklist

Use this checklist quarterly to review and harden your online digital footprint across all social media platforms (LinkedIn, Instagram, X/Twitter, Facebook, TikTok, Reddit, GitHub).

---

## Category A: Profile Visibility & Discoverability
- [ ] **Profile Access:** Set default account visibility to "Private" or "Friends Only" where feasible.
- [ ] **Search Engine Indexing:** Disable *"Allow search engines outside this platform to link to your profile"*.
- [ ] **Contact Identifier Lookup:** Set *"Who can look you up using your email/phone"* to "Nobody" or "Friends Only".
- [ ] **Friends & Followers Visibility:** Restrict follower list visibility to "Only Me" or mutual contacts.

---

## Category B: Personal Information & PII Minimization
- [ ] **Phone Number Exposure:** Remove public phone numbers from your bio, headline, and profile fields.
- [ ] **Email Exposure:** Hide personal email address; use a privacy relay or dedicated alias for inquiries.
- [ ] **Birth Date Exposure:** Conceal your birth year entirely; set day/month visibility to "Only Me".
- [ ] **Residence Exposure:** Remove exact street or neighborhood details; limit location to general country or region.
- [ ] **Family Relationships:** Disconnect public relationship linkages and family tree listings.

---

## Category C: Location Privacy & Geotagging
- [ ] **Real-Time Check-ins:** Avoid real-time location tags and live venue check-ins.
- [ ] **Travel Itineraries:** Post vacation photos and trip milestones only after returning home safely.
- [ ] **Camera Geotagging:** Disable GPS location permissions for camera apps in mobile OS settings.
- [ ] **Routine Patterns:** Avoid sharing daily commute routes, recurring gym times, or predictable jogging tracks.
- [ ] **Landmark Hygiene:** Check background details before posting to ensure street signs and house numbers are obscured.

---

## Category D: Content & Workplace Security
- [ ] **Workplace ID Badges:** Never photograph or share corporate ID cards, badges, lanyards, or building access cards.
- [ ] **Computer Displays:** Ensure office monitors, code editors, and desk whiteboards are blurred or cropped out.
- [ ] **Minors & Schools:** Avoid publishing photos of minors in school uniforms or identifying school crests.
- [ ] **Physical Tokens:** Keep boarding passes, flight tickets, house keys, and credit cards strictly off camera.

---

## Category E: Connections & Followers
- [ ] **Connection Vetting:** Decline or verify connection requests from unfamiliar profiles.
- [ ] **Contact Pruning:** Conduct a quarterly connection audit to remove dormant or unverified profiles.
- [ ] **Engagement Pods:** Avoid follow-for-follow schemes and engagement pods that inject botnet monitors.

---

## Category F: Tagging & Mentions
- [ ] **Tag Review Queue:** Enable *"Review posts you are tagged in before they appear on your profile"*.
- [ ] **Tagging Permissions:** Restrict who can tag you in photos to approved friends only.
- [ ] **Public Mentions:** Restrict `@mention` permissions to mutual connections.

---

## Category G: Authentication & Account Security
- [ ] **Multi-Factor Authentication:** Enable MFA using a hardware key (YubiKey) or authenticator app (TOTP). Avoid SMS 2FA.
- [ ] **Unique Passwords:** Ensure every social platform uses a unique, 16+ character password stored in a password manager.
- [ ] **Login Alerts:** Turn on alerts for unrecognized devices and logins from new IP addresses.
- [ ] **Active Sessions:** Review the *"Where you are logged in"* list and terminate old, inactive sessions.
- [ ] **Recovery Data:** Ensure recovery email and mobile numbers are current and themselves protected by MFA.

---

## Category H: Third-Party Applications & OAuth
- [ ] **App Audit:** Review authorized third-party apps under *"Apps & Websites"* in settings.
- [ ] **Revoke Stale Tokens:** Remove legacy games, personality quizzes, and unneeded integrations.
- [ ] **Least Privilege:** Decline apps requesting excessive scopes such as contact book or direct message access.

---

## Category I: Messaging & Anti-Phishing Hygiene
- [ ] **OTP Confidentiality:** Never disclose one-time SMS verification codes or passcodes to anyone over chat.
- [ ] **DM Links:** Treat unsolicited links or compressed files sent via direct message as malicious.
- [ ] **Biographical Quizzes:** Decline engagement posts asking for childhood trivia, first pet names, or street names.
- [ ] **Impersonation Verification:** Call friends or colleagues directly to confirm urgent requests for funds or gift cards.

---

## Category J: Digital Footprint & Account Lifecycle
- [ ] **Legacy Post Archival:** Use platform tools to archive or delete timeline posts older than 12-24 months.
- [ ] **Zombie Accounts:** Identify and formally delete dormant profiles on legacy platforms (Tumblr, Ask.fm, etc.).
- [ ] **Self-OSINT Audits:** Search your own name and handles in search engines every 6 months to detect data broker leaks.
